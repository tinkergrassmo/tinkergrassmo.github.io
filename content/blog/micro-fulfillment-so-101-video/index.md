  <div class="blog-intro-copy">
    <p><span lang="en"><strong>First end-to-end autonomous run — live.</strong> After weeks of system integration, calibration, and bench testing, our <strong>SO-101 robotic arm</strong> is officially picking, sorting, and kitting travel toiletries on its own.</span></p>
    <p><span lang="en">This is our <strong>first official working demo</strong>. The setup is intentionally lean, the trajectories are conservative, and we sped the footage up 2x to keep the pace brisk—but the execution is 100% autonomous, and seeing the system close the loop for the first time is a milestone worth celebrating.</span></p>
    <p><span lang="zh-Hant"><strong>首個端到端自主運行——正式上線。</strong> 經過數週的系統整合、校準與實機測試，我們的 <strong>SO-101 機械手臂</strong>正式實現自主抓取、分揀與組裝旅行裝護理用品。</span></p>
    <p><span lang="zh-Hant">這是我們的<strong>首個官方實機展示</strong>。目前的測試環境刻意保持精簡，運動軌跡也採取保守設定，影片亦以 2 倍速呈現以保持節奏明快——但每一個動作皆為 100% 自主執行。見證系統首度完整閉環運作，是值得團隊自豪的重要里程碑。</span></p>
    <h2><span lang="en">What You're Seeing</span><span lang="zh-Hant">展示內容</span></h2>
    <p><span lang="en">Three pick-and-place cycles. The arm reaches out, grasps lightweight cylindrical bottles and rectangular soap cases, and places each item accurately into a kitting tray. Condensed into a 43-second clip at 2x speed, every trajectory is generated and executed autonomously with zero manual intervention.</span><span lang="zh-Hant">三輪完整的取放循環。機械手臂伸展、抓取輕量圓柱形瓶罐與方形皂盒，並將每件物品精準放置於組裝托盤中。影片以 2 倍速濃縮為 43 秒，所有軌跡均由系統自主生成並執行，全程無任何人工干預。</span></p>
    <h2><span lang="en">Why This Milestone Matters</span><span lang="zh-Hant">這個里程碑的意義</span></h2>
    <ul>
      <li><strong><span lang="en">Reliable handling of mixed geometries:</span><span lang="zh-Hant">穩定處理混合幾何形狀：</span></strong> <span lang="en">Lightweight, low-friction packaging with varying form factors is notoriously unforgiving. The SO-101 transitions cleanly between cylindrical and rectangular items without crushing or slipping.</span><span lang="zh-Hant">輕量、低摩擦力且形狀各異的包裝向來極具挑戰。SO-101 能夠在圓柱形與方形物件之間流暢切換，精準夾持而不造成擠壓變形或滑脫。</span></li>
      <li><strong><span lang="en">Compact tabletop footprint:</span><span lang="zh-Hant">緊湊的桌面級佔地空間：</span></strong> <span lang="en">The entire cell operates on a standard workbench—making it directly viable for space-constrained environments like hospitality back-of-house operations or micro-fulfillment stations.</span><span lang="zh-Hant">整套工作站僅需一張標準工作台的空間即可運作——這使其能直接導入空間受限的商業場景，例如飯店後勤備品區或微型配送站。</span></li>
    </ul>
    <h2><span lang="en">What's Next</span><span lang="zh-Hant">下一步規劃</span></h2>
    <p><span lang="en">Validating the baseline pipeline is just step one. Over the coming weeks, we're pushing cycle times with smoother velocity profiles, expanding our object taxonomy, and integrating closed-loop visual perception for unstructured bin picking. We'll continue sharing engineering updates as the system evolves.</span><span lang="zh-Hant">驗證基礎管線只是第一步。接下來幾週，我們將透過更平滑的速度規劃縮短循環時間、擴展可處理的物件類別，並整合閉環視覺感知以應對非結構化料箱抓取。隨著系統持續演進，我們也會定期分享最新的工程進展。</span></p>

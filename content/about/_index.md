## Tinkergrass | 元野有限公司

<p lang="en">Tinkergrass is building a complete, sustainable, and highly intelligent industrial ecosystem centered on robotic automation.</p>

<p lang="zh-Hant">元野有限公司致力打造以機器人自動化為核心，完整、可持續且高度智能化的工業生態系統。</p>

## Project | 項目

**Robotics Industrial Ecosystem | 機器人工業生態系統**

<p lang="en">The project aims to make robotic automation easier to deploy and more capable in real production settings, while reducing interruptions through intelligent fault detection and recovery.</p>

<p lang="zh-Hant">本項目旨在降低機器人自動化的部署難度，提升機器人在實際生產環境中的應對能力，並透過智能故障檢測與恢復減少生產中斷。</p>

## Mission | 使命

<p lang="en">We are creating a new industrial paradigm centered on robotic automation. We focus on labor shortages and work that is difficult for people to carry out because of its hazards or process complexity.</p>

<p lang="zh-Hant">我們開創以機器人自動化為核心的工業新範式，致力填補勞動市場缺口，並解決因高危險性或工藝複雜等因素而難以由人力完成的工作。</p>

## Core Capabilities | 項目特色

### Accessible Setup | 簡易部署

<p lang="en">Configure a robotic system quickly without requiring a deep professional coding background.</p>

<p lang="zh-Hant">無需深厚的專業程式背景，即可輕鬆上手，快速完成機器人系統配置。</p>

### Flexible, Precise Handling | 靈活精準的物料操作

<p lang="en">Adapt to changing production conditions and carry out complex industrial material handling steadily, precisely, and efficiently.</p>

<p lang="zh-Hant">靈活應對多變的實際生產條件，穩定、精準且高效地執行各種高度複雜的工業級物料操作。</p>

### Autonomous Recovery | 自主故障恢復

<p lang="en">Intelligent fault detection and recovery reduce equipment downtime and maintenance, helping keep production running continuously.</p>

<p lang="zh-Hant">透過智能自主故障檢測與恢復，減少設備停機與維護時間，協助生產線持續穩定運轉。</p>

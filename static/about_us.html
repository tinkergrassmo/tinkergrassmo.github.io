About Us關於我們

We earned PhDs in Robotics from Cornell University and have spent more than a decade developing robotics hardware and software in Silicon Valley and other leading technology hubs. From taking technologies from zero to one at startups to scaling products and bringing them to market at multinational companies and technology leaders such as Johnson & Johnson and Intrinsic (Alphabet), our work spans surgical robotics, smart agriculture, industrial manufacturing, and service robotics. We bring this experience from academia and industry together to make advanced robotics part of everyday life.我們畢業於美國康奈爾大學（Cornell University）並取得機器人學博士學位。過去十多年來，我們深耕於美國矽谷及頂尖科技重鎮，專注於機器人軟硬體系統的開發。從在初創企業中帶領團隊實現技術的「從零到一」，到在跨國企業與科技巨頭（如 Johnson & Johnson 及 Alphabet 旗下的 Intrinsic）中推動產品的規模化與商業落地，我們的足跡橫跨了醫療手術、智慧農業、工業製造及生活服務等多個領域。我們將這些橫跨學術界與產業界的寶貴經驗匯聚於此，致力於將尖端的機器人技術真正融入並改變大眾的日常生活。

The Value We Bring我們能帶來的價值

Our team brings more than 15 years of robotics research, development, and deployment experience, specializing in motion planning, robot-arm control, algorithm development, and low-level hardware and software integration. We combine deep technical expertise with commercial experience: we know how to rapidly iterate prototypes with limited resources at a startup and how to build highly reliable systems for large enterprises. From system architecture and hardware-in-the-loop testing to seamless integration into real customer environments, we can turn concepts into mature products ready for commercial use.我們團隊擁有超過十五年的機械人研發與實際部署經驗，專精於運動規劃、機械臂控制、演算法開發及底層軟硬件整合。我們不僅具備尖端的技術研發基礎，更擁有商業實戰觸覺。我們深明如何在初創環境中以有限資源快速迭代開發原型，同時亦懂得為大型企業建立高可靠性系統。從架構設計、硬件在環測試到終端客戶場景的無縫整合，我們擁有將概念轉化為成熟產品的完整技術能力，確保解決方案能真正落實商業化應用。
